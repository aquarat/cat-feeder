## Superseded assumptions and open dependencies

**D13 — evidence-driven next step:** do not freeze the square-cap layout or
order twelve thermal stacks from nominal TEC power alone. The reduced-order
model finds warmer remote food regions even with an ideal 0°C cap. Longer
stock-size footprints improve coverage, but none establishes a reliable
all-food target. Latest D19 replaces the stock-cap trial with direct TEC contact;
compare one real wedge under an insulated lid before scaling procurement. See
the thermal model's assumptions and comparisons.

- Do not revive twelve commercial cups, a custom 300 mm aluminium spreader disc, foam insulation or a purely lift-off lid as settled requirements.
- The brief's two-TEC wiring, two large heat sinks and thermal/power estimates belong to its old architecture. Reassess against the distributed candidate; twelve CAD positions do not establish that twelve modules fit its 100 W supply budget.
- The brief's 40 mL fill, cold-food target, ambient qualification condition and 180 mm printer envelope are starting targets, not achieved results. Actual useful capacity and full assembly printability remain to be demonstrated.
- Priorities for resolving D08: real stock component dimensions/availability, independently supported dry thermal cartridges, cleanable moisture separation, then measured contact/drag and thermal results. Consult [mechanical review](mechanical-review.md) and [thermal model](thermal-model.md).
