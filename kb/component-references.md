# Component evidence and sourcing status

Read 2026-10-24. These are sizing references, not an approved shopping list.

## Historical stock cold cap — removed from current prototype

[Custom Thermoelectric CP-1.63-1.63-AL-01](https://customthermoelectric.com/cold-plate-1-63-x-1-63-x-12.html)
is a catalogue 6061 aluminium cold plate, **41.28 × 41.28 × 3 mm**, 14 g,
specified for 40 mm TECs. The supplier states a smooth surface and a sensor hole.
This establishes that a ready-made small cap exists without custom disc machining.

The page showed USD 13.75 each, USD 11.80 at ten, **only five in stock**. It is a
US supplier; UK availability, delivery, tax and enough stock for twelve remain
unconfirmed. Earlier CAD used its envelope; owner decision D19 now removes it
and tests direct TEC ceramic-to-PETG contact. This source remains historical
evidence that catalogue plates exist, not an active purchasing requirement.

## TEC mounting

[Ferrotec installation guide](https://thermal.ferrotec.com/technology/thermoelectric-reference-guide/thermalref06/)
requires flat, parallel interfaces and appropriate compression, warns against
structural/shear loading through the ceramics, and discusses moisture sealing.
It also explains thickness matching when several TECs share rigid plates.

The guide does not establish that soft PETG sliding on the ceramic will damage
this particular assembly. The owner accepts that contact for the prototype;
inspect actual drag, wear and contact rather than treating fracture as certain.
Independent modules still need repeatable face height, flat hot-side contact and
support. A printed locating pocket is not itself a compression clamp or moisture
seal. Final retention remains screwless; the practical trial can precede a
qualified long-life retaining mechanism.
