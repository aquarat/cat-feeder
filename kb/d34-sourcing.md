## How the numbers were obtained

- **uk.rs-online.com, docs.rs-online.com, Farnell search, DigiKey, Mouser and Octopart refuse scripted
  access (HTTP 403 / bot pages).** RS stock numbers, UK GBP price breaks, sales packs and public stock
  figures were taken from the findchips.com (Supplyframe) distributor feed, which carries RS UK and
  Farnell UK rows. The raw rows used are frozen in
  [`sourcing/findchips-2026-10-26.json`](../electronics/d34/sourcing/findchips-2026-10-26.json).
  Specifications for a few RS-only lines came from RS's public export mirror (export.rsdelivers.com).
- Stock figures are a snapshot, not a reservation. **Re-check every line in a logged-in browser before
  ordering.**
- Prices are GBP **ex VAT** per piece at the break reached by the packs bought; delivery is not included.
  Two lines have no visible UK price and use RS's export-site EUR price × 0.87 (marked in the table).
- [`electronics/d34/sourcing.py`](../electronics/d34/sourcing.py) rebuilds the table from `design.py`,
  `catalog.py`, the snapshot and `sourcing/manual.json` →
  [`bom-sourced.csv`](../electronics/d34/sourcing/bom-sourced.csv) / `.json` / `.md`.

- **JST VH has no wire-to-wire plug** (eVH: wire-to-board only), so the harness's "VHP-2N" TEC junctions
  do not exist. Use **Molex Mini-Fit Jr 2-way wire-to-wire**: 39-01-2020 receptacle + 39-01-3023 plug

- **Cold-zone 3-way inline:** JST SM pre-wired pairs (Adafruit 1663, The Pi Hut), 22 AWG, latching;
  device tails are soldered and heat-shrunk. RS has SM housings but only loose contacts.
- **Hall switch: TI DRV5033AJQLPG** (RS 900-9807), not DRV5023 (not stocked in the UK). Same TO-92 pinout
  (1 VCC, 2 GND, 3 OUT), 2.5–38 V, open drain, but omnipolar. It works unchanged with ctrl J3
  (3.3 V supply, 10 k pull-up R15, 1 k series R16): **no circuit change.**
- **Lid reed** (RS PRO KSK-1A66, RS 265-2226) switches LID to GND against the existing 10 k pull-up;
  **basin-full probe** (two stainless pins) pulls LEAK to GND through the water against 47 k; ADC reads via
  10 k. DC at ≤ 70 µA on stainless pins: corrosion expected to be slow; firmware should sample briefly
  [estimate]. **No circuit change.** Both share the ctrl J4 SENSE plug.
- **24 V brick: MEAN WELL GST160A24-R7B** (160 W, 6.67 A, meets D32's ≥ 150 W; Farnell 2815854, not at RS)

**No UK maker is cheaper than JLCPCB; the cheapest UK-made option costs about 15–30× more.**

**Recommendation: PCB Train (Newbury Electronics), standard 15-working-day service, immersion silver,
about £340 inc VAT** if UK manufacture is the priority. It meets every rule the cards use, and its published
minimums are all looser than the JLCPCB recommended rules the cards already pass. Immersion silver
solders well by hand but should be assembled within about 6 months or kept sealed. Possible saving to ask
them about: all three designs as one 81 × 145 mm board (£144.45 at 15 WD, about £179 inc VAT) if they will
accept separate circuits on one panel. **If UK manufacture is not required, JLCPCB remains the obvious
choice** (the JLC zips are still generated).

Upload package per card: `electronics/d34/<card>/<card>-pcbtrain.zip` (RS-274X without X2 attributes,
Excellon PTH + NPTH, `README.txt` with stack-up, finish, outline and file list). PCB Train requires no
particular file names. `electronics/d34/pcbtrain-2layer.kicad_dru` encodes their published minimums;
`export.py` runs it as a second DRC (`validation.json`: `DRC_pcbtrain`).
